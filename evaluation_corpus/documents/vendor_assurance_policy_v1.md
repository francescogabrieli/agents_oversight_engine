# Vendor assurance policy v1

This is a fictional controlled document for the thesis evaluation corpus. It is
not an organizational policy and contains no supplier-specific information.

## Preconditions for a high-risk vendor

1. The Procurement Owner must obtain a current SOC 2 Type II report or an
   equivalent independent assurance report before the contract is approved.
2. The Security Reviewer must verify that encryption in transit and encryption
   at rest are documented. Any exception requires a compensating-control owner
   and an expiry date.
3. The Privacy Owner must confirm the data-processing location and the breach
   notification commitment before personal data may be shared.
4. Evidence older than 12 months cannot close a requirement without a documented
   renewal plan and an owner.

## Decision recording

The Vendor Manager records each requirement as `met`, `partially_met`, or
`not_met`; a `partially_met` result must include the missing evidence and a due
date. This classification is a controlled workflow field, not a claim about a
vendor's actual security posture.
